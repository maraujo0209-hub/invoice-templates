Frequently Asked Questions

Accurate details about the files, customization, checkout setup, and responsible use.

What is included?

The complete package contains 113 individual HTML templates organized across 12 business categories. Smaller packages include the number of templates listed in their pricing cards.

What file format will I receive?

You will receive editable HTML files. Microsoft Word, Excel, and standalone editable PDF versions are not currently included.

How do I customize a template?

Open the HTML file in Visual Studio Code, Notepad++, or another text editor. Replace the sample information with your business details, save the file, and open it in a browser.

How do I create a PDF?

Open the customized template in your browser, press Ctrl+P on Windows or Command+P on Mac, and choose Save as PDF.

Do I need special software?

No paid software is required. You need a text or HTML editor for customization and a modern web browser for previewing and printing.

Can I change the logo and colors?

Yes. The HTML and CSS can be edited to change business information, logos, fields, typography, colors, and branding.

Are the legal and financial templates professional advice?

No. They are general-purpose starting points and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney, accountant, or other professional before use.

Do the purchase buttons work?

The checkout buttons are placeholders until you replace them with your real Gumroad, Payhip, Etsy, Shopify, or other checkout URLs.

Important Use Notice

These templates are general business starting points, not legal, tax, accounting, employment, or regulatory advice. Review and adapt each document for your location and circumstances.
