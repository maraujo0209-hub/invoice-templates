This Operating Agreement governs the internal operations of [Company Name, LLC] and is entered into by the Members listed below.

Definitions

Unless otherwise defined, the following terms have the meanings given below wherever they appear in this Agreement:

"Agreement"This document, including all exhibits, schedules, and amendments.
"Effective Date"The date this Agreement is signed by both parties, as stated on the signature page.
"Confidential Information"Any non-public business, technical, or financial information disclosed by either party.
"Parties"The individuals or entities identified on the cover page of this Agreement.
1. Formation & Purpose

State the company's formation date, state of organization, and business purpose.

2. Membership Interests

Describe each member's ownership percentage and capital contributions.

3. Management

Describe whether the LLC is member-managed or manager-managed, and the authority of managers.

4. Profit & Loss Allocation

Describe how profits, losses, and distributions are allocated among members.

5. Transfer of Interests

Describe restrictions on members selling or transferring their ownership interest.

6. Dissolution

Describe the events that would trigger dissolution of the LLC and how assets would be distributed.

LLC governance requirements vary by state. Have this agreement reviewed by a business attorney before use.
